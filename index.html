How to spot a suspicious email

  1. Check the sender. Carefully inspect the full email address, not just the display name.
  2. Inspect the destination. Hover over a link on a computer or press and hold it on a phone to preview its destination. Do not open it if it looks unfamiliar.
  3. Be cautious of urgency. Unexpected threats, deadlines, invoices, and account-verification requests can be used to pressure you.
  4. Never share credentials. Do not provide passwords or MFA codes through links in unexpected messages.
  5. Verify through another channel. Contact the sender using a known number or address, or ask the IT Department.
  6. Report suspicious messages. Follow VTT's internal process for reporting suspicious emails.

Remember

A familiar logo, convincing wording, or a message that appears to come from a colleague does not automatically make a link safe.

Pause. Check. Verify. Report.
